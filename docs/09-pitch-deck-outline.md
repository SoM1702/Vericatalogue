# Pitch Deck Outline (7 Slides)

**Slide 1: Title & Tagline**
- **Title**: VeriCatalog Proof
- **Tagline**: Evidence-First Product Intelligence for Industrial Commerce.
- **Subtext**: Making AI output auditable and PIM-ready.

**Slide 2: The Problem**
- AI extraction is powerful but untrustworthy for industrial specs.
- A hallucinated material or pressure rating breaks commerce and trust.
- Data stewards waste time manually verifying AI outputs instead of approving them.

**Slide 3: The Solution (VeriCatalog Proof)**
- A "Trust Layer" that sits before the PIM.
- Extracts and normalizes data, but most importantly: attaches irrefutable evidence to every attribute.
- Routes conflicts and missing data to a human-in-the-loop Workbench.

**Slide 4: Key Differentiators**
- **Not a Chatbot**: Structured, schema-driven intelligence.
- **Evidence-First**: Field-level lineage (source file, page, snippet).
- **Rule-Based Normalization**: Deterministic validation layers on top of AI extraction.
- **Bounded Review Agent**: Four inspect-only local tools turn exceptions into an auditable, ranked human queue—without autonomous edits.

**Slide 5: How It Works (Architecture Flow)**
- Visual flow: Supplier PDF/CSV -> Extraction & Normalization Engine -> Validation & Evidence Linking -> Bounded Evidence Review Agent -> Human Review Workbench -> PIM-ready JSON/CSV.

**Slide 6: Hackathon Demo Scope**
- Focused on Industrial Valves & Fittings.
- Demonstrating unit normalization, conflict resolution, and batch catalog health scoring.
- Completely local and reproducible.

**Slide 7: Business Impact**
- Demonstrate a locally runnable, evidence-first review workflow and submission-ready materials.
- Show formula-defined completeness, conflict, missing-field, and review-priority metrics for the processed synthetic demo data.
- Validate next with an authorized labelled supplier dataset; do not claim unmeasured time savings, accuracy, ROI, or “100%” results.
